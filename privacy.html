Privacy Policy

Content Machine - Last updated September 2026

Content Machine is operated for personal use by its owner. It does not collect, sell, or share data about the public. This policy explains how it handles the platform accounts the owner chooses to connect.

What is accessed

  • Access tokens for platforms the owner explicitly connects, such as YouTube or TikTok, used only to upload video content to that owner's own account.
  • Basic profile information, such as account name, needed to confirm which account is connected.

What is not done

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No accounts are accessed other than the ones the owner directly connects.
  • No content is read from or posted to any account without the owner's action.

Storage

Connection tokens are stored locally by the application for as long as the connection remains active, and are removed when the owner disconnects the account.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the last-updated date above reflects the most recent version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the account owner directly.
